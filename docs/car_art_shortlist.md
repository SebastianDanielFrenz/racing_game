Research date 2026-10-14. Method: Sketchfab public API (via WebFetch, filter downloadable=true, license=cc0/by), kenney.nl, quaternius pages, WebSearch.

## Limits of this research (read first)
- **No file could be downloaded from this container.** curl through the agent proxy returns `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403` for sketchfab.com, api.sketchfab.com, kenney.nl, opengameart.org, itch.io, polyhaven.com, fab.com, blendswap.com, 3d.si.edu (egress policy; not retried). So **no samples are in samples/**, and "wheels separate", "interior", "PBR resolution" and "formats" could NOT be verified by opening files. Where stated, they come from the model page text/tags and are marked "claimed"; otherwise "unverified".
- Sketchfab downloads always need a (free) login or API token; glTF/USDZ/source archives are offered once logged in. Direct anonymous download: NO for every Sketchfab entry. Kenney/Quaternius/OpenGameArt/itch: direct, no login (kenney.nl has a "continue without donating" link), but not testable here.
- Sketchfab "faceCount" = triangles. Data from the API on 2026-10-14.
- Licence texts: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ ; CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
- Poly Haven: no car models found (their models are props/nature). Smithsonian/Open3D: CC0 but scans of museum objects (e.g. Cleveland Museum of Art toy "1939.678 Streamline Automobile", wood toy, 100k tris, CC0) - not usable as drivable cars. Fab: free-listing pages could not be read (licence field not rendered), so no Fab entry is recommended; Fab "Poly Cars Pack" (polyperfect, stylised, 200+ cars) exists but licence/triangles unverifiable here. BlendSwap/ambientCG: nothing suitable found (ambientCG has no models).
- Trademark column: "none" = fictional/generic; "RISK" = real brand/model.

## Coverage vs. requested types
- City car: Ace '11 (#1), Kenney/Quaternius (stylised).
- GT/sports/muscle: Phoenix 455 (#2), Muscle California Speed (#9).
- Vintage roadster: only real-brand options found (Model T roadster #11, Honda S800 #15, plus very heavy 1-3 M-tri Kryox Shade/OggPhish scans). Gap: no fictional CC0/CC-BY roadster found. Cheapest fix: commission/keep generating in-house (tools/cargen Runabout T) or unbrand Model T (1908-27 design).
- Hypercar: Future Car (#8) fictional; Formula One LP-830 (#10) is open-wheel. Gap for a road-legal fictional hypercar with interior.
- Sedan: Fairheaven LT (#3).
- Traffic: Zhabotinsky set (#3-#6: sedan, wagon, police, box truck, van, ambulance, hatch), Comrade1280 pack (#7), Kenney, Quaternius.

## Top 5 recommendation
1. **Daniel Zhabotinsky generic car family (#1-#6), CC BY 4.0** - one author, one visual style, ~19-26k tris, explicitly generic/fictional names, author gives written permission to use/modify/ship in digital products; covers city car, sedan, wagon, cop, muscle, van, truck, ambulance. Best traffic fleet; attribution line needed (add to data/credits.json). Verify wheels/interior after download (low-poly: expect simple interior).
2. **Muscle (California Speed), ViktorKom, #9** - the only candidate that claims all of: interior, rigged suspension with separate wheels, 4K/2K PBR sets, 130k tris, game-ready. Best hero/GT candidate; check that the body is generic enough (trademark) and verify the claims first.
3. **Future Car, 3DHaupt, #8** - fictional hypercar/concept, 130k tris, rigged in Blender, 6 textures; fills the hypercar slot. Credit the original concept designer too; interior/wheel separation unverified.
4. **Comrade1280 Generic passenger car pack, #7** - 10 unbranded body types with colour variants in one download (~7k tris each) for far/background traffic and LODs; CC BY 4.0.
5. **Kenney Car Kit + Quaternius Low Poly Cars (#12/#13), CC0** - no attribution, direct downloads, usable immediately as placeholder/low-LOD or stylised traffic; the only fully login-free options. Style differs from the realistic rest, so use only if a stylised look is acceptable.
